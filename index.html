Current work

  • Measurement — hidden error in LLM evals & benchmarks and how to reduce it. Measuring the spread of ideas across languages with fine-tuned LLMs; estimating ideology via forced-choice LLM-as-judge.
  • AI & Democracy — what LLMs refuse and the political consequences. How state media in training data shapes model output. Alignment choices and democratic outcomes.
  • Economic impacts — simulating real-world economic tasks to understand model capabilities and labor-market impacts.
